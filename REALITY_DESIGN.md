# Reality is a thin thing

The site is now a stack of five surfaces. A diagonal fold exposes a sliver of the next world. Pull it and the current layer peels away; release too soon and it settles back. Going upward folds the previous world back over the current one.

The surface is charcoal and pale green, with oversized literary typography against the existing filament artwork. Under it are Minerva's deep green, the author's copper atmosphere, violet machinery, and a cold blue open channel. The artwork and book respond to the soundtrack, but sound is optional.

## Explore

- Pull the illuminated seam left, or tap it.
- Use Right / Page Down to descend; Left / Page Up to return.
- Scroll past a layer's content to descend. Long content scrolls normally first.
- Select any depth from the bottom markers or Layers menu.
- Start Minerva Down from the persistent player. Seek and mute work across layers.
- Choose Reading mode for the whole document, or Still the world to stop ambient motion.

## Local validation

JavaScript syntax and whitespace checks pass. A Node-based controller harness verifies forward/reverse transitions, full and partial drags, canceled gestures, direct layer jumps, keyboard isolation for inputs, reading mode, and still mode. Local image, script, soundtrack, archive, and game references exist.

Browser verification after repairing the depth-counter selector confirmed all five layers, a pointer drag, forward/reverse transitions, and retention of all page sections. The Minerva layer was also inspected visually. This is not a full device audit. This version is for local design review and has not been committed, pushed, or deployed.
